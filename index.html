Yes. I build your new homepage from what's already on your site and your Google profile. If you don't like it, you owe nothing, and you can keep the ideas.

It depends on how many pages you need and what the site has to do. You'll get one fixed price on our call, after you've seen your mockup. No surprises after that.

AI writes the code, which is why it's fast. The design, words and layout are built around your real photos, reviews and services, and you see it before you pay.

You get a preview link before launch and one full round of changes. Small edits after launch are easy to arrange.
